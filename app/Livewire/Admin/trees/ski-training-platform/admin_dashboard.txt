Platform Admin[color:red]:Run the platform and manage the organisations who use it.
	Dashboard[color:pink]:See how the business is doing at a glance.
		Active Organisations:How many orgs are using the system right now.
		Revenue Overview:What's been invoiced and what's outstanding.
		Total Coaches:How many coaches are active across all organisations.
	Content & Products[color:green]:What you sell to organisations.
		Exercise Library:Create and manage the global pool of exercises.
		Program Library:Create and manage the global pool of programs.
		Products:Bundle exercises, programs, and features into packages.
			Free Bundles:Applied to all organisations by default.
			Paid Bundles:Organisations can opt in and pay for these.
			Custom Bundles:Bespoke combinations built for specific organisations.
	Customers & Billing[color:blue]:The organisations who use the system and how they pay.
		Organisation List:See all your customers and their status.
			Usage Overview:Coaches and activity per org.
			Assigned Bundles:What each org has access to.
			Price Overrides:Discount or free access for specific orgs.
		Onboarding:Steps to get a new org up and running.
			1. Create Organisation:Set up the org account.
			2. Assign First Coach Admin:Create their first coach admin login.
			3. Pick Bundles:Assign free, paid, or custom bundles.
			4. Send Welcome Credentials:Email login details to the new org.
		Invoices:All invoices, paid and outstanding.
		Send Reminders:Chase up unpaid invoices.
		Payment History:Track what's been paid and when.
	Platform Management[color:purple]:Keep the platform running and configured.
		Features:All features and their availability per organisation.
		Authentication:Login, password reset, and role management.
		Languages:Supported languages (DE, FR, IT, EN).
		Support & Documentation:Help centre, user guides, and support channel.
		Operations:Server health, monitoring, deployments, and bug tracking.
		Settings:Default features, email templates, and global configuration.
