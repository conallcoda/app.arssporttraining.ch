Coach Dashboard[color:orange]:Create and manage training schedules for your athletes.
	My Team[color:blue]:Manage your coaches, athletes, and training groups.
		Coaches:Add and manage coaches with colour-coded identification.
		Athletes:Manage athlete profiles and assign them to coaches.
			Performance Tracking:Track 1RM, readiness, and other metrics over time.
		Groups:Organise athletes into training groups.
	Exercise Library[color:green]:Browse, create, and organise exercises.
		Exercises:Create exercises with videos, categories, and equipment.
			Exercise Settings:Configure defaults for each exercise.
				Sets & Deload:Set count with optional odd/even week deload.
				Reps:Manual targets or automatic step-down progression.
				Weight:Manual targets or automatic 1RM-based progression.
				Rest, Duration & Tempo
				Heart Rate & HR Zones:Manual targets or automatic calculation.
				Distance, Pace & Watts
			Photos & Video:Upload photos or link YouTube demos.
			Live Preview:See how settings play out across weeks and sessions.
		Templates:Save exercise settings as reusable presets.
		Categories:Organise exercises into a category tree.
		Equipment & Modifiers:Tag exercises with equipment and variations.
		Import:Browse and import from an external exercise library.
	Workouts[color:teal]:Build training programs from your exercise library.
		Programs:Assemble exercises into reusable workout programs.
			Exercise Order & Supersets:Drag to reorder and group exercises together.
			Warm-Up & Cool Down:Link separate programs for warm-up and Cool Down.
			Week-by-Week Grid:View the full program laid out across weeks and sessions.
		Training Categories:Colour-coded types like Strength, Hypertrophy, and Conditioning.
	Schedule[color:purple]:Plan and visualise when your athletes train.
		Overview:Colour-coded grid showing all groups at a glance.
		Day / Week / Month View:View and quick-add sessions into AM or PM slots.
		Training Phases:Block-based planning for periodised training.
			Phase Setup:Create training blocks with date ranges and goals.
			Exercise Grid:Week-by-week, set-by-set view of every exercise.
				Edit Cells:Adjust individual sets, reps, or weight per week.
				Copy Weeks:Duplicate a week's pattern across the program.
				Automatic Weight Targets:Calculate weights from athlete 1RM data.
			Athlete Customisation:Override phase dates or goals for individual athletes.
			Notes & Annotations:Add focus notes and deload markers to the timeline.
